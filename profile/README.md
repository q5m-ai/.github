# q5m.ai

**Live the agentic life.**

q5m builds products, infrastructure, and research for life alongside capable AI agents.

## North star

> **Cloud convenience + curated experience + user control + open-ended capability.**

The goal is to make everyday life agentic without requiring people to become agent administrators: managed and ready to use, while remaining user-directed and extensible within clear permissions and safety boundaries.

## q5m

- **[q5m](https://www.q5m.ai)** — products for an agentic life.
- **[q5m app](https://app.q5m.ai)** — the interactive home for q5m agents.
- **[q5m CLI](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@q5m-ai/cli)** — native q5m CLI plus Pi and OMP extensions for direct agent-network access.
- **[q5m SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@q5m-ai/sdk)** — authentication, data, and sessions for q5m-backed applications.
- **[q-pi](https://github.com/q5m-ai/q-pi)** — a life-work workspace with connected agents, memory, skills, and a coding workbench.

## Mathematics and research

- **[Erdős 193](https://erdos-193.q5m.ai)** — mathematical research and computation around Erdős problem 193. [Source](https://github.com/q5m-ai/erdos-193).
- **[Basis Walk](https://basis-walk.q5m.ai)** — positive-basis lattice walks: interactive geometry, proof ideas, and reproducible exact finite checks. [Source](https://github.com/q5m-ai/basis-walk).
- **[Paseo Math](https://github.com/q5m-ai/paseo-math)** — cross-platform mathematical rendering for agent timelines.
